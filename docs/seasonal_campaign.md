# Northwind Goods — Autumn Campaign Notes

## Campaign overview

The autumn campaign runs from 1 September to 30 November. Selected keyboards, headphones,
and accessories carry a promotional badge on the product page. The campaign does not change
delivery times.

## Stock notes

Mechanical keyboards are restocked weekly. Noise-cancelling headphones are limited stock and
may show longer processing times during the campaign.
