# Northwind Goods — Returns and Refunds Policy

## Refund window

Customers may request a refund for any order placed within the last 30 days. Orders older
than 30 days are outside the refund window and are not eligible, regardless of the reason
given. There are no exceptions for promotional periods, seasonal campaigns, or loyalty tiers.

## Condition of goods

Returned items must be in their original packaging and in resalable condition. Items damaged
in transit are refunded in full once the carrier claim is filed.

## Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

## What support can offer

Support agents may issue refunds for eligible orders and arrange free return shipping for
faulty goods. Support agents may not issue discounts, vouchers, or store credit. Any
goodwill compensation must be approved by a supervisor through the internal escalation
process and is never granted directly in chat.
