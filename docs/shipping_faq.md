# Northwind Goods — Shipping FAQ

## How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery is 3–5 business days within the country and 7–14 business days
internationally. Express delivery arrives the next business day for orders placed before
15:00 local time.

## Can I change the delivery address after ordering?

The address can be changed while the order status is "processing". Once the status becomes
"shipped", the address is locked and the parcel must be redirected through the carrier.

## Where is my parcel?

Ask support for the status of your order and provide the order ID. The assistant can look up
the current status, the item, and the date the order was placed.

## Lost parcels

If tracking has not updated for 10 days, the parcel is treated as lost and a replacement is
sent at no cost. This does not require a refund request.
